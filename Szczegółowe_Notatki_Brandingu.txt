Example user interface: The page looks modern, but also like from a IT student that likes minimalism. The page starts with a "Welcome, would you like to pivot your life?" text and a sign in or sign up and also other text that promote neuroeducation about health and awareness of the significance of being healthy and how it aligns with the goal in other areas of life. Depending on the choice, then we have a page with "Welcome back" or "Welcome to 'no bs'" letters only. Click anywhere on the page to proceed. The same looking page, but this time it asks: "What would you like to focus on?" aligning with the 'no bs' philosophy. every word and design choice should align with 'no bs' philosophy. We have vertical grid with 3 choices: "Sleep", "Habits" and "Eating". If we click "Sleep", then we go to the appropriate page for working on sleep, then a questionnare checking the current state of sleep related things, to then later based on that, set an alert "Turn off electronics at [1h before bedtime]" and "Wake up at [waking time]", if the user choose to enable notification in the "Profile" page. A "list" page is a page about 21-day challenge of habit tracking 6 habit, where it's expected for you to do only 4 of those. You can check out the habit like a to-do task. The rule is to be consistent, no lost day compromising. There's a progress bar and a pleasant sound after checking off the habit. After the questionnare, we are transitioned to the "recommended" page, where we have a headline quote of a short fact and maybe pictures that's digestible, there's 'read more' depending if the text is longer than one sentence and should have a body, if the user clicks it, then the text 'opens up' like the title quote: "Sleep is the cost of being awake" if clicked on 'read more' then it's followed by body: "Sleep resets focus, makes you more alert and emotionally stable. You tend to feel sleepy when the temperature is falling [..] For every 1h of extra sleep, a woman gets her interest in becoming sexually with her partner increases by 14%." and below that are references to the scientific paper or a quote from a scientist it was based on and based on that quote we can add the habit to the "list" and name it "Sleep". 5 more habits to choose. We can also delete the habit we don't want to do anymore, but before that, there's a pop-up to confirm the choice. Up on the page we have 3 pages to choose from: "list" page, "recommended" page and "profile" page after logging in or "about" page if you're not logged in. The profile page has sections in respective order: "you"( your username, you can also change password), "stats" (Your time on the website, your habits on the calendar, the calendar only shows 7 days and number of the day and month is also written if you finished the day with 4 or more habits, you can get a star on this day), "settings" (change appearance to light or dark mode, set notification and alarm for sleep and waking up time, set volume), "about us"( redirects to the "about us" page) and "suggestions".

The "Waking Up" Transition: In Home.razor, when ActiveView changes, do not swap instantly. Implement a CSS transition where the old view fades out and scales down (0.98) while the new view fades in and scales up (0.98 to 1.0).
